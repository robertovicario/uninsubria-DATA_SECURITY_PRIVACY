# Data Security

Security deals with protecting data from ***unauthorized access, modification, and disruption***. Its main goal is to ensure that ***data remain confidential, accurate, and available*** to authorized users.

## CIA Triad

The CIA triad is a widely ***used model that guides organizations*** in their data security practices. It consists of three core principles:

- **Confidentiality:** Ensuring that sensitive information is accessed only by authorized individuals.
- **Integrity:** Maintaining the accuracy and consistency of data over its lifecycle.
- **Availability:** Ensuring that data is accessible to authorized users when needed.

Some ***additional security properties*** could be considered:

- **Authenticity:** Verifying the ***identity of users and systems*** to prevent unauthorized access.
- **Accountability:** Ensuring that actions can be traced back to the responsible party, often through ***logging and auditing***.
- **Privacy:** Protecting ***personal and sensitive information*** from unauthorized access and disclosure.

## Threats

A threat is a potential danger to an asset that may lead to a ***violation of security***. One important category of threats is ***malware***, and the main types of malware are:

- **Virus:** A type of malware that ***attaches itself to a legitimate program*** or file and spreads to other programs or files when executed.
- **Worm:** A standalone malware that ***replicates itself to spread to other computers***, often exploiting vulnerabilities in network protocols.
- **Trojan:** A type of malware that ***disguises itself as a legitimate program*** or file to trick users into executing it.
